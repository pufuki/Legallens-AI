PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT

This Partnership Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of September 1, 2025 (the
"Effective Date"), by and between GreenTech Solutions LLC, a Massachusetts LLC ("GreenTech"),
and EcoVentures Inc., a Massachusetts corporation ("EcoVentures"). The parties are referred
to individually as a "Partner" and collectively as the "Partners."

1. PARTNERSHIP PURPOSE
The Partners form a partnership to develop and market sustainable energy products and
related technologies. The partnership shall operate under the name "GreenEco Alliance."

2. CONTRIBUTIONS
GreenTech shall contribute $150,000 USD and proprietary solar panel technology. EcoVentures
shall contribute $150,000 USD and marketing expertise. Additional contributions require
unanimous consent.

3. PROFIT AND LOSS
Profits and losses shall be shared equally (50/50) between the Partners. Distributions shall
be made quarterly within 30 days of the end of each quarter.

4. MANAGEMENT
Each Partner has equal management rights. Major decisions require unanimous consent,
including: (a) entering contracts exceeding $50,000 USD; (b) hiring key personnel;
(c) acquiring or disposing of assets; (d) incurring debt.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All intellectual property developed by the partnership shall be jointly owned by the
Partners. Pre-existing IP remains the property of the contributing Partner. Each Partner
grants the partnership a license to use their pre-existing IP for partnership purposes.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each Partner shall maintain the confidentiality of partnership information. This
obligation survives for 5 years after termination.

7. LIABILITY
Each Partner shall be liable for their own negligence. The partnership shall maintain
general liability insurance of $2,000,000 USD.

8. TERM AND TERMINATION
The partnership term is 5 years. Either Partner may terminate with 120 days written notice.
Upon termination, assets shall be liquidated and proceeds distributed per the profit ratio.

9. NON-COMPETE
During the partnership and for 12 months after, neither Partner shall engage in a competing
sustainable energy business without consent.

10. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement.
