COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Commercial Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into as of August 1, 2025 (the
"Effective Date"), by and between Property Holdings LLC, a Colorado corporation (the
"Landlord"), and StartupLab Inc., a Colorado corporation (the "Tenant").

1. PREMISES
The Landlord leases to the Tenant approximately 5,000 square feet of office space located
at 1234 Innovation Drive, Suite 200, Denver, Colorado (the "Premises").

2. TERM
The lease term is 36 months commencing August 1, 2025 and ending July 31, 2028.

3. RENT
The Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $12,500 USD, due on the 1st of each month. Rent shall
increase by 3% annually. Late rent incurs a $200 USD late fee plus 1% interest per month.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $25,000 USD upon signing. The deposit shall be
returned within 30 days of lease termination, less any deductions for damages.

5. USE OF PREMISES
The Premises shall be used for general office purposes only. The Tenant shall not use the
Premises for any unlawful or hazardous purpose.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
The Landlord shall maintain the building structure and common areas. The Tenant shall
maintain the interior of the Premises and pay for any damage caused by the Tenant.

7. LIABILITY AND INSURANCE
The Tenant shall maintain liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 USD and name the
Landlord as an additional insured. The Landlord shall not be liable for injury to the
Tenant's property or persons.

8. TERMINATION
The Tenant may terminate early with 90 days notice and payment of a termination fee equal
to 2 months' rent. The Landlord may terminate for material breach with 30 days notice.

9. GOVERNING LAW
This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of Colorado.
