FREELANCER AGREEMENT

This Freelancer Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of October 1, 2025 (the
"Effective Date"), by and between MediaGroup Inc., a California corporation (the "Client"),
and Alex Rivera, an individual freelancer (the "Freelancer").

1. SERVICES
The Freelancer shall provide graphic design and branding services, including logo design,
brand guidelines, and marketing collateral as described in the Statement of Work.

2. TERM
This Agreement covers the period from October 1, 2025 to December 31, 2025, unless extended
by mutual agreement.

3. PAYMENT
The Client shall pay the Freelancer $75 USD per hour, with a project cap of $15,000 USD.
The Freelancer shall submit invoices bi-weekly. Payment is due within 15 days of invoice.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
Upon full payment, all intellectual property in the deliverables shall transfer to the
Client. The Freelancer retains the right to display the work in their portfolio. The
Freelancer's pre-existing tools and templates remain the Freelancer's property.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY
The Freelancer shall keep confidential all information provided by the Client. This
obligation survives for 2 years after termination.

6. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR
The Freelancer is an independent contractor, not an employee. The Freelancer is responsible
for their own taxes, equipment, and insurance.

7. REVISIONS
The Freelancer shall provide up to 3 rounds of revisions per deliverable at no additional
cost. Additional revisions are billed at $75 USD per hour.

8. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate with 7 days written notice. Upon termination, the Client shall
pay for all completed work. The Freelancer shall deliver all work-in-progress.

9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
The Freelancer's liability shall not exceed the total fees paid under this Agreement.

10. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement.
