VENDOR AGREEMENT

This Vendor Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of July 1, 2025 (the "Effective
Date"), by and between RetailCo Inc., a Nevada corporation (the "Buyer"), and SupplyChain
Ltd., an Arizona corporation (the "Vendor").

1. SUPPLY OF GOODS
The Vendor shall supply the Buyer with electronic components as described in the purchase
orders issued by the Buyer from time to time. The Vendor shall deliver goods within 14 days
of receiving a purchase order.

2. PRICING
Prices are set forth in the pricing schedule attached as Exhibit B. The Vendor shall provide
a 5% volume discount for orders exceeding $50,000 USD. Prices are fixed for 12 months.

3. PAYMENT TERMS
The Buyer shall pay all invoices within 45 days of receipt (Net 45). Invoices exceeding
$100,000 USD are payable within 60 days.

4. QUALITY AND WARRANTY
The Vendor warrants that all goods shall conform to specifications and be free from defects
for 24 months from delivery. The Vendor shall replace defective goods at no cost within
10 days of notification.

5. INDEMNIFICATION
The Vendor shall indemnify the Buyer against claims arising from defective goods, including
product liability claims and intellectual property infringement.

6. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
The Vendor's liability for defective goods shall not exceed the purchase price of the
defective goods. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party shall protect the other's confidential information, including pricing, product
designs, and business plans. This obligation survives for 5 years.

8. TERM AND TERMINATION
This Agreement is for 3 years and automatically renews for 1-year terms. Either party may
terminate with 90 days notice. The Buyer may terminate immediately for material breach or
insolvency of the Vendor.

9. FORCE MAJEURE
Neither party is liable for delays caused by events beyond their reasonable control.

10. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Nevada.
