EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into as of January 15, 2025
(the "Effective Date"), by and between TechCorp Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "Company"),
and Jane Smith, an individual residing in California (the "Employee").

1. POSITION AND DUTIES
The Company hereby employs the Employee as Senior Software Engineer. The Employee shall
perform the duties customary to such position and such other duties as may be assigned
by the Company. The Employee shall report to the Chief Technology Officer.

2. COMPENSATION
The Company shall pay the Employee an annual base salary of $145,000 USD, payable in
bi-weekly installments. The Employee shall be eligible for an annual performance bonus
of up to 15% of base salary, payable within 30 days of the end of each fiscal year.

3. CONFIDENTIALITY
The Employee acknowledges that during employment, the Employee will have access to
confidential information, including trade secrets, source code, customer lists, and
business strategies. The Employee agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all such
information during and after employment. This obligation shall survive termination
indefinitely.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All work product, inventions, and intellectual property created by the Employee during
the course of employment shall be the exclusive property of the Company. The Employee
hereby assigns all right, title, and interest in such intellectual property to the Company.
This assignment is irrevocable.

5. NON-COMPETE
For a period of 12 months following termination of employment, the Employee shall not
engage in any business that competes with the Company within the United States. This
restriction applies to direct and indirect competition.

6. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time with 30 days written notice.
The Company may terminate the Employee immediately for cause, including gross misconduct,
fraud, or material breach of this Agreement. Upon termination, the Employee shall receive
all accrued salary through the date of termination.

7. BENEFITS
The Employee shall be eligible for health insurance, 401(k) with 4% company match, and
15 days of paid time off per year.

8. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. Any disputes
shall be resolved in the courts of Delaware.

9. NOTICE
All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the parties at
the addresses listed above.
