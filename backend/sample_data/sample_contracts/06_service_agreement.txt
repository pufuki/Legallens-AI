SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of June 1, 2025 (the "Effective
Date"), by and between Global Services Inc., a Florida corporation (the "Provider"), and

1. SERVICES

infrastructure, including 24/7 helpdesk support, on-site visits, and system monitoring.

This Agreement is for 24 months commencing on the Effective Date, ending June 1, 2027.

3. PAYMENT
The Client shall pay $8,500 USD per month, invoiced on the 1st of each month and payable
within 15 days. A late fee of 2% per month applies to overdue invoices.

4. SERVICE LEVELS
The Provider shall respond to critical issues within 2 hours and non-critical issues within
8 hours. The Provider targets 99.5% system uptime.

5. INDEMNIFICATION
The Provider shall indemnify the Client against third-party claims arising from the
Provider's negligence or willful misconduct.

The Provider's total liability shall not exceed $50,000 USD. The Provider shall not be
liable for indirect or consequential damages.

7. WARRANTY
The Provider warrants that services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike
manner. The Provider shall re-perform any deficient services at no additional cost.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party shall protect the other's confidential information. This obligation survives
for 3 years after termination.

9. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate with 60 days written notice. The Client may terminate
immediately for material breach. Upon termination, the Provider shall transition services
within 30 days.

10. FORCE MAJEURE

including natural disasters, strikes, and government actions.
