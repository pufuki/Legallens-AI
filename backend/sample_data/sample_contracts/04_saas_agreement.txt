SAAS SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This SaaS Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of April 1, 2025
(the "Effective Date"), by and between CloudApp Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "Provider"),
and Customer Corp, a New York corporation (the "Customer").

1. SERVICES
The Provider grants the Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access and
use the CloudApp platform (the "Service") for the Customer's internal business operations
during the subscription term.

2. SUBSCRIPTION AND FEES
The Customer shall pay an annual subscription fee of $24,000 USD, billed in monthly
installments of $2,000 USD. Invoices are payable within 15 days of receipt. Late payments
incur a 1.5% monthly interest charge.

3. TERM AND RENEWAL
The initial term is 12 months commencing on the Effective Date. This Agreement shall
automatically renew for successive 12-month terms unless either party provides written
notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before the end of the current term.

4. DATA PROTECTION
The Provider shall process Customer data in accordance with applicable data protection
laws, including GDPR and CCPA. The Provider shall implement reasonable security measures
to protect Customer data. The Provider shall not use Customer data for any purpose other
than providing the Service.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of the other party's confidential information.
This obligation survives for 3 years after termination.

6. SERVICE LEVELS
The Provider targets 99.9% uptime. If the Service is unavailable, the Customer may request
service credits equal to 10% of the monthly fee for each full day of downtime.

7. WARRANTY
The Provider warrants that the Service will perform substantially in accordance with the
documentation. The Service is provided "as-is" and the Provider disclaims all other
warranties, express or implied.

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Each party's liability shall not exceed the fees paid in the 12 months preceding the claim.
Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages.

9. TERMINATION
The Customer may terminate for material breach with 30 days notice. The Provider may
terminate immediately for non-payment or material breach. Upon termination, the Provider
shall provide 30 days of data export access.

10. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
The Provider retains all intellectual property in the Service. The Customer retains all
intellectual property in its data.

11. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
