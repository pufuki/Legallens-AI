CONSULTING AGREEMENT

This Consulting Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of May 20, 2025 (the
"Effective Date"), by and between Enterprise LLC, an Illinois corporation (the "Company"),
and Michael Chen, an individual consultant (the "Consultant").

1. SERVICES
The Consultant shall provide strategic advisory services related to market expansion and
operational efficiency, as further described in Exhibit A. The Consultant shall report to
the Chief Executive Officer.

2. TERM
This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for 6 months, ending on
November 20, 2025, unless terminated earlier.

3. COMPENSATION
The Company shall pay the Consultant a monthly retainer of $12,000 USD, payable on the 1st
of each month. The Consultant shall submit monthly invoices. Additional work beyond the
scope shall be billed at $200 USD per hour.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY
The Consultant shall maintain the confidentiality of all proprietary information disclosed
by the Company. This obligation survives for 5 years after termination.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All deliverables created by the Consultant for the Company shall be the property of the
Company. The Consultant assigns all rights in such deliverables to the Company. The
Consultant retains ownership of pre-existing methodologies and tools.

6. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR
The Consultant is an independent contractor and not an employee. The Consultant is
responsible for their own taxes and insurance. This Agreement does not create a partnership
or joint venture.

7. NON-COMPETE
During the term and for 6 months after, the Consultant shall not provide consulting services
to any direct competitor of the Company within the same industry.

8. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement with 14 days written notice. Upon termination,
the Consultant shall be paid for all services rendered through the termination date.

9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
The Consultant's liability shall not exceed the fees paid in the 3 months preceding any
claim.

10. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois.
